Privacy Policy & Child Safety

Our Commitment to Child Safety

At Punnapra Sixes (Six S Punnapra), the safety, privacy, and well-being of young athletes is our top priority. We steer youth away from mobile addiction, drugs, and negative habits through healthy sports on the court.

Information We Collect

When parents or guardians submit our registration form, we collect only minimal, essential details:

  • Parent or guardian's full name
  • Child's name or initial
  • Contact phone number

How Information is Handled

All registration responses are kept strictly confidential and accessible exclusively by designated, authorized club officials. We never sell, share, or disclose children's personal information to commercial third parties, marketers, or advertisers.

Photos & Media Consent

No identifiable photographs or full names of minors are published in our public gallery without express consent from their parent or legal guardian.

Data Removal & Inquiries

Parents may request the deletion or correction of their family's information at any time by contacting our officials directly or emailing contact@sixspunnapra.com.
