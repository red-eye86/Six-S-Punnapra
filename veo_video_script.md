# Six S Punnapra: Promotional Video Script & Veo Prompts

Since Veo generates short, high-quality clips (usually around 5-10 seconds each), the best way to create a 1-2 minute promotional video is to generate a series of connected scenes and stitch them together in a video editor (like CapCut, Premiere Pro, or even built-in phone editors).

Here is a scene-by-scene guide with specific Veo 3 prompts and suggested voiceover/text overlays to create a powerful story about the club's revival.

---

## Part 1: The Legacy & The Call (0:00 - 0:15)
*Theme: Nostalgia, history, and the need for a comeback.*

**Scene 1 (0:00 - 0:05): The Historic Roots**
* **Veo Prompt:** Cinematic wide shot, golden hour. An old, dusty, but beautiful sandy field in Kerala, India near a historic church cemetery. Warm sunlight filtering through palm trees. Nostalgic, vintage film look, slow panning motion.
* **Suggested Text/Voiceover:** "For generations, the grounds of Punnapra held our stories..."

**Scene 2 (0:05 - 0:10): The Modern Problem**
* **Veo Prompt:** Cinematic medium shot, modern day. A group of teenagers sitting indoors on a couch, heads down, glued to glowing smartphones. Cold blue lighting contrasting with the warm outside light. Slight slow-motion.
* **Suggested Text/Voiceover:** "...but somewhere along the way, we traded the field for screens."

**Scene 3 (0:10 - 0:15): The Awakening**
* **Veo Prompt:** Cinematic close up. A pair of worn-out sports shoes stepping firmly onto red clay soil. Dust kicks up in slow motion. Dynamic low angle, high energy, crisp 4k resolution.
* **Suggested Text/Voiceover:** "It's time to step back out."

---

## Part 2: The Revival & Action (0:15 - 0:45)
*Theme: High energy, showcasing the sports, and the new infrastructure.*

**Scene 4 (0:15 - 0:22): Volleyball Action**
* **Veo Prompt:** High-speed action shot. A volleyball player leaps high into the air at the net for a powerful spike. Outdoor court, bright sunny day, dynamic camera angle following the action. Sweat glistening, intense focus.
* **Suggested Text/Voiceover:** "The Six S Club is back. And the game is on."

**Scene 5 (0:22 - 0:30): Football Training**
* **Veo Prompt:** Cinematic tracking shot at ground level. A soccer ball being skillfully dribbled through grass by young athletes in training gear. Sun flare through the trees, vibrant green colors, energetic and fast-paced motion.
* **Suggested Text/Voiceover:** "Professional training for the youth..."

**Scene 6 (0:30 - 0:37): Cricket Pitch Preparation**
* **Veo Prompt:** Cinematic wide shot. A lush green cricket pitch being rolled and prepared. In the foreground, a cricket ball spins gracefully in the air in extreme slow motion. Sunny afternoon, sharp focus.
* **Suggested Text/Voiceover:** "...world-class pitches being prepared..."

**Scene 7 (0:37 - 0:45): Badminton Intensity**
* **Veo Prompt:** Indoor dramatic lighting. A fast-paced badminton rally, shuttlecock flying past the camera in slow motion. Athletes lunging. High contrast lighting, neon accents, intense sports photography style.
* **Suggested Text/Voiceover:** "...and indoor courts designed for champions."

---

## Part 3: The Community & The Future (0:45 - 1:05)
*Theme: Unity, health, and a call to action.*

**Scene 8 (0:45 - 0:52): The Community United**
* **Veo Prompt:** Cinematic wide shot. A diverse crowd of people in a Kerala village setting—elders, youths, and children—cheering together on the sidelines of a sports match. Joyful expressions, vibrant colors, sunset lighting, panning shot.
* **Suggested Text/Voiceover:** "More than just sports. We are building a healthier generation."

**Scene 9 (0:52 - 1:00): The Next Generation**
* **Veo Prompt:** Cinematic close up. A young child, smiling brightly, holding a sports ball, looking up with hope and excitement. Warm, soft lighting, blurred background (bokeh), uplifting atmosphere.
* **Suggested Text/Voiceover:** "A stronger, united Punnapra."

**Scene 10 (1:00 - 1:10): Outro / Logo Reveal**
* **Veo Prompt:** Abstract cinematic shot. Glowing, electric blue and neon orange light streaks forming dynamic shapes on a dark background. (Note: You can overlay your actual Six S logo and website on top of this background in your video editor).
* **Suggested Text/Voiceover:** "Join the movement. Visit sixspunnapra.com."

---

### Tips for Generating in Veo:
1. **Consistency:** Add keywords like `cinematic, 4k, photorealistic, Kerala setting, vibrant colors` to the end of every prompt to ensure the style looks consistent across all clips.
2. **Motion:** Veo responds well to motion commands. Use words like `slow motion, panning shot, tracking shot, zoom in` to control how the camera moves.
3. **Editing:** Once you generate these 10 clips, drop them into a video editor, add an inspiring background music track (search for "cinematic sports background music"), and add the text over the video!
